ABOUT

Elly Moghtaderi (b. Tehran) is a filmmaker based in Waterloo, Canada. As a self-taught artist, her practice relies on collaboration and post-disciplinary research. Currently, she is working on the short film, ALAMOUT PROJECT, the last installment of her short film trilogy.

Through her works, she explores the occultural form of moving images by repurposing them as a heterogenoues fabulating force. These fabulations act as double exposures, folding trans/queer ontologies unto middle-eastern landscapes. The goal is to establish a haptic window in our collective visual memory, a collective memory that's haunted by violent historical traumas. Mythologizing as an alternative path for future(s) to exist. A historical-materialist coping mechanism that doesn't rely on negation.
